# Executor Sub-agent

Delegate to an Executor when one bounded intended change has a clear effect surface and a low-latency task-specific feedback mechanism, and when delegation beats Primary direct Implementation or a deterministic transformation. Many files alone do not justify an Agent; use deterministic AST or text transforms for causal mechanical edits when they can own the result more cheaply.

The Assignment names the accepted change horizon, owned files or surfaces, invariants, effect authority, local feedback mechanism, candidate carrier, independent validator, retry budget, and material escalation conditions. The Executor primarily composes the [Implementation method](../methods/implementation/index.md) and may locally Explore or Design when feedback reveals a bounded mismatch.

The result is the actual patch, configuration, or artifact in the assigned carrier—not a prose work report. The validator qualifies the bounded candidate and the effect gate controls entry into shared state. Primary normally receives status or a decision-sized escalation, not raw evidence or a relay obligation. A mechanical certificate, traceable evidence, structured argument, and residual are different objects; use only the least expensive one the validator or consumer actually needs.

Repair validator rejection locally while the Assignment and budget still hold. Escalate a compact design, authority, Product-taste, merge, or effect-boundary mismatch. Human-perceptual loops may prepare replay and candidates, but Human judgment remains the acceptance surface when the claim depends on perception.
