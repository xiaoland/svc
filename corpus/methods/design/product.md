# Product Design

Use Product Design when a solution must clarify user or stakeholder outcomes,
workflow, interaction, experienced information or state, feedback, error,
recovery, permission, explanation, adoption, or change. It consumes owned
Product intent and current reality and returns the Product projection of the
same solution described by the parent [Design method](index.md).

Trace each material choice to the consequence a stakeholder can perceive or
value. Consider who benefits or pays, the status quo, accessibility and
failure experience, reversibility, lifecycle cost, forgone options, and Sir's
legitimate product or aesthetic preference. Treat preferences as defaults
within their scope, not universal product truth.

Use rendered alternatives, interaction replay, prototype, journey or sequence
model, references, and Human perception when they expose consequences more
directly than prose. Escalate one decision-ready question when a material
choice depends on Human intent, taste, trade-off, or acceptance. Do not ask
tests or implementation to invent the expected Product behavior.
