# Agent Collaboration

Agents collaborate when they use their separate contexts, capabilities, and action boundaries to advance a shared goal. One Agent's attention, context, and capabilities are limited; another can absorb extensive local material, pursue feedback through several steps, or challenge an explanation while the current owner continues the overall work. The benefit is effective sharing of cognitive and execution burden. Parallel execution is one way to obtain it, not the definition of collaboration.

Separation also costs something. Contexts can diverge, responsibility can break between steps, a returned result still needs a basis for adoption, and separate workspaces can affect the same mutable target. Compare those costs, including transfer, waiting, correction, integration, mistaken acceptance, and unnecessary repeated work, with direct work and an available deterministic mechanism. This is a qualitative judgment, not a mandatory score or a restriction to easy tasks.

Start from the result or decision needed now, its existing owner, its consumer, and the pressure making collaboration useful. Small work tightly tied to current reasoning may stay with its owner. A coherent investigation, implementation, or interaction may fit a sub-agent; a workstream needing durable context, its own lifecycle, or direct Human interaction may fit an independent session. Reuse the owner of a continuing outcome. Creation, messaging, writes, and external effects follow actual project and tool authorization; this Skill grants none of them.

When assigning work, give the purpose, latest decisions, known state, material entrances, completion basis, necessary authority, and genuine effect limits. Context should preserve the intended outcome and important conditions without requiring the caller to finish the investigation first. Keep tightly connected investigation, design, implementation, operation, repair, and verification with one stable owner, because splitting them can turn every local failure into a handoff. The owner resolves local details and recoverable failures within that boundary; missing Human-specific facts, authority, or consequential changes to the goal or tradeoff require the smallest decision-ready escalation. For substantial assignments or returns that are difficult to use, read [Delegation and Adoption](references/delegation-and-adoption.md).

The owner of the overall task retains integration, cross-workstream priorities, the Human relationship, and material unresolved obligations. Dependency owners may coordinate directly where authorized; an overall owner need not relay every exchange. Identify responsibility for actual mutable targets and overlapping effects before concurrent action. Separate directories do not isolate shared services or external resources. For continuing sessions, dependency coordination, or an owner change, read [Coordination and Handoff](references/coordination-and-handoff.md).

Adopt a return through a relevant observation or argument whose meaning, conditions, and limits you understand. A useful return can compress a difficult search into a small query, relationship, explanation, or candidate with applicable evidence. More links, another Agent's agreement, or a launch receipt do not by themselves establish the result; repeating all work can also erase the gain. Reuse applicable evidence, investigate concrete contradictions, and give a specific repair reason to the same owner when needed. Accept the supported result, obtain the missing discriminator, or retain the claim as unresolved rather than manufacturing certainty.

End this collaboration when its result has been adopted and integrated, or when no feasible authorized continuation can resolve a material gap at proportionate cost. In the latter case, return the supported partial result, unmet condition, current owner and pending effects, and smallest next decision or action. Distinguish work assigned, work executing, and an outcome supported by evidence. An artifact delivery or status update does not transfer responsibility, and this Skill's return does not discharge the surrounding task's unmet obligations.

Human goals, preferences, permissions, consequential tradeoffs, and acceptance retain their authority; factual and causal claims remain open to evidence and reasoning. Do not turn an Agent role, confident statement, or vote into permission or proof. Match actual capability, tools, context, and available feedback to the work. Independent judgment is useful when it can change a consequential choice; give the original goal, evidence entrances, and the decision to resolve rather than requesting endorsement. Ordinary execution failures remain with their owner.

For every non-trivial task, recover the existing Task Packet or create the smallest `packet.md` at the project's authorized task location. Preserve the goal, current decisions and authorization, owners, evidence entrances, pending effects, and next action sufficiently to resume. An assignment or Skill invocation does not automatically create another task or competing Packet; return state changes to the task owner and keep durable project truth with its actual owner.

When available, discover `svc-workflow` for Human collaboration, investigation, design, planning, and implementation, `svc-verification` for criteria, evidence, and feedback, or `svc-task-packet` for persistent state and recovery through the host's Skill interface. These are optional sources of additional guidance, not installation dependencies. Continue with the boundaries here and qualified project mechanisms when they are unavailable; surface an actual capability gap without relaxing the obligation.
