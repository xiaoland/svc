# Handoff

Read this as the current or prospective owner when responsibility must change or work must recover after interruption or lost contact. It expands the entry's continuity guidance; establish an accepting successor and ownership of every pending effect.

Transfer for a reason to replace the owner, not merely an artifact delivery or recoverable failure. Preserve current Human decisions and authority, effective progress, evidence entrances, work in progress, remaining obligations, shared targets, and in-flight operations. Make the transfer recoverable in the existing Task Packet without competing state or authority. Conclude it only when the successor accepts continuation and responsibility for pending effects is explicit.

A missing reply establishes neither cancellation nor stopped effects. Inspect saved work and actual operations before assigning the same effects again: an operation may continue after its Agent stops. Stop, wait for, or coordinate it as the environment requires before a successor repeats it. If its state cannot be established, report that uncertainty and choose an authorized continuation that does not assume it ended.

A transfer does not complete the Task. Preserve unresolved obligations and inform the Human of material changes in ownership or completion state. If continuation remains blocked, return supported progress, the missing condition, current owner, pending effects, and the smallest useful decision or action.
