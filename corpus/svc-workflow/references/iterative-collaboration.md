# Iterative Collaboration

Use this reference when a project or Task agreement or direct Human instruction selects iterative collaboration. It organizes bounded results around experience and learning while retaining known authority; otherwise Workflow's staged default applies.

## Choose the Next Useful Increment

Recover the purpose, current usable state, latest feedback, decisions and reasons, and authorization. Apply the shared judgment to select a bounded result that advances the purpose or tests a consequential premise: an investigation, a design comparison, or a realized behavior. Choose its feedback from the distinction it should reveal.

Actual use can clarify emerging needs. Select an increment that makes the relevant consequence observable and limits dependent commitments while the premise is provisional.

## Learn with the Human

Deliver a result the consumer can use, with applicable evidence. Where preference, comprehension, or interaction matters, provide an experience entry and a focused reason to examine it. Complete feasible engineering and product-behavior observations yourself and ask the Human for needed experience judgments.

Interpret feedback through the shared purpose and the authority for its effects. An agreed setting disappearing after login calls for repair within existing authority. Sharing it between accounts changes ownership and privacy choices and needs a proposal. Use feedback to revise the understanding, increment, or route, applying the authorization appropriate to affected work.

Propagate a revised choice through its affected work, preserving valid results and evidence. Keep prototype observations and approved production behavior distinct. Continue unaffected authorized work while a consequential Human choice is pending.

## Deliver and Continue

Stop an increment when its intended result and relevant evidence are ready; integrate them and identify the next useful gap when the Task continues. Update existing Task state.

Continue under the agreed collaboration rhythm until the Human selects a change. Complete necessary authorized work and finish the Task when the agreed result and remaining obligations are addressed. If a prerequisite prevents continuation, return the supported state and smallest missing condition.
