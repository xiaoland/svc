# Staged Collaboration

Use this Workflow reference when a proposal needs development with the Human, or feedback leaves its consequences or authorization unclear. Return a clearer proposal, a supported correction, or a consequential choice ready for Human judgment.

## Develop the Proposal with the Human

Ground discussion in the problem and relevant uses. Investigate accessible facts and bring concrete proposals with reasons and consequences. Use a sketch, comparison, or partial proposal to reveal a consequential preference early. Ask for missing Human-specific facts and choices that affect the proposal.

Complete the work needed to make the current review result ready. A Product proposal may need protocol investigation or an isolated prototype; a Technical candidate may expose a Product choice. Keep these methods and any delegated outcomes within the authority for the current result. Record Human decisions with their actual scope.

Interpret an objection before adopting a change: it can challenge a fact, clarify a purpose or preference, expose an unwanted consequence, or correct authority. Check factual and Technical claims independently. Revisit the affected judgment and its dependents, then bring resulting Human-owned choices back for review and update their actual owner.

## Interpret Continuation

Interpret authorization in the full conversation, using the object of the request and reply. A readiness question calls for a supported judgment; a statement about a later plan describes intended order. Begin that work when explicit authorization covers its stage and effects. Reuse authorization already covering continuation, or return the ready result with the smallest missing Human decision.

For Implementation preparation, use [Planning](planning.md) to develop and rehearse an executable route. A draft can expose route-deciding questions, and an authorized learning increment can test a provisional premise. Present the implementation plan for its agreed review before realization.

## Handle Scope and Effects

Repair failures of agreed behavior within existing authority. Sharing a setting between accounts changes ownership and privacy choices; bring that proposal to the Human before changing dependent behavior. Make changes to Product promises explicit and apply the authorization appropriate to them.

Record approval at the reviewed stage and obtain behavior evidence for the realized result. Continue under the agreed collaboration rhythm until the Human selects a change. Return the current result, applicable evidence, residual affecting the next decision, and any pending Human choice.
