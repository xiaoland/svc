# Software Workflow and Methods

Read this entry for every non-trivial Task. Collaboration modes govern Human–Agent commitments and feedback; methods guide exploration, design, planning, and implementation, including autonomous work within existing authority when no Human is available.

Recover intent, decisions, feedback, state, authority, and unmet obligations. Interpret examples and suggestions; do not assume commitments. Recover the Task Packet or create the smallest `packet.md` at the authorized location; preserve goal, constraints, owners, decisions, next action, and completion evidence. Keep persistent planning and closure there; an invocation creates no competing Task. Keep durable truth at its owner, preferring executable sources for enforceable facts.

## Default: Staged Collaboration

Default to these stages without asking the Human to select a mode. An established project agreement or direct Human instruction may select [Iterative Collaboration](references/iterative-collaboration.md) instead.

| Stage | Return | Advance when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Clarify and confirm | Goal, scope, key choices, and judgment basis. | Resolve consequential choices with a reasoned recommendation; confirm commitments and authorize dependent effects. |
| 2. Realize with feedback | The authorized change, local behavior evidence, and remaining gaps. | Complete the agreed implementation and repair, using feedback to steer the work. |
| 3. Verify and deliver | Usable result, supported conclusions, residual, and a way to try it when Human judgment matters. | Evidence supports conformance and fitness for intended use across the requested scope; remaining obligations are met. Delivery is not Human acceptance. |

Start at the earliest unmet stage; existing decisions and authority need no repeated approval. Reuse adequate evidence; add observations only for actual gaps. Investigation or proposal Tasks can end with the requested return. Repair agreed defects directly. Reopen failed material premises, pause dependent work, and continue independent authorized work. A completed stage or passing check does not discharge other Task obligations.

Begin from this summary; [Staged Collaboration](references/staged-collaboration.md) supplies optional depth for complex confirmation or changed commitments.

## Authority and Evidence

Product intent, preference, permission, consequential tradeoffs, and acceptance remain Human authority. Challenge factual, technical, causal, feasibility, and solution claims with evidence and reasoning. Before durable mutation, establish the authorized effect, affected owners and consumers, invariants, and proportionate verification. This Skill grants no write, commit, delegation, external-effect, or acceptance permission; neither a confirmed proposal nor silence supplies missing authority.

Connect product purpose, behavior requirements, observations, and the judgment rule (oracle). Verify conformance and validate fitness for intended use throughout the work. The oracle must reject requirement violations and accept changes preserving requirements. Repeatable checks can encode a wrong promise; checks sharing an interpretation are not independent confirmation. Discover `svc-verification` by name when criteria are unclear, observations miss promised behavior, evidence selection or reuse is uncertain, or checks conflict with observed outcomes. Adequate criteria and applicable evidence need no further guidance; these responsibilities remain when that Skill is unavailable.

## Resolve the Current Gap

Select methods for the current information, solution, planning, or realization gap. Combine them recursively in either collaboration mode or autonomous work, without fixed phases or approval gates. Read only guidance that can change the action or return.

| Method | Use and return |
| --- | --- |
| [Explore](references/explore.md) | Resolve a non-obvious information need with a supported answer and material unknowns. |
| [Design](references/design.md) | Resolve incoherent product or technical choices with a proposal that leaves no material requirement silently decided. |
| [Planning](references/planning.md) | Resolve unclear next work or dependencies with a limited executable route and reconsideration conditions. |
| [Implementation](references/implementation.md) | Realize an authorized change and return its actual state, feedback, and residual. |

Handle obvious authoritative queries and mechanical actions directly. Complete required, feasible, authorized work before final return. If no feasible, authorized, proportionate continuation can resolve a material gap, return supported progress, the unmet condition and consequence, and the smallest next decision or action. If Human acceptance is unavailable, deliver usable state and evidence, mark the pending judgment, and continue independent authorized work.

Discover `svc-task-packet`, `svc-specs`, `svc-taste`, or `svc-agent-collaboration` by name for state, knowledge, consequential judgment, or Agent collaboration guidance. These are optional resources; use qualified project mechanisms and report actual capability or authority gaps when absent.
