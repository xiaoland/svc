# Templates

## Purpose and Use

This catalog provides reusable Consumer document shapes when a recurring
consumer needs a stable starting contract. Load a template only after the
document's semantic owner and creation pressure are known; an existing
template is not evidence that a Consumer needs the file.

## Ownership and Boundary

Templates own slot names, short completion guidance, and the boundary of the
document they instantiate. The consuming project owns the completed content,
authority, and verification. This catalog does not own product truth, task
state, workflow selection, automatic scaffolding, or completed-work logs.

## Selection

Use [`task-packet/index.md`](task-packet/index.md) for the opt-in Task Packet
family. `svc task init` creates only that family's `packet.template.md` shape;
all other family members require their own admitted topology or information
pressure. For independent Consumer surfaces, select the matching atomic shape:

- [`AGENTS.root.template.md`](AGENTS.root.template.md) — project-root
  instructions and owner map.
- [`AGENTS.local.template.md`](AGENTS.local.template.md) — additive subtree
  instructions and tripwires.
- [`alignment-change-request.template.md`](alignment-change-request.template.md)
  — a bounded cross-repository alignment request.
- [`deployment-runbook.template.md`](deployment-runbook.template.md) — an
  incident's operational mitigation and rollback contract.
- [`edit-shared-docs.template.md`](edit-shared-docs.template.md) — a Spoke
  protocol for returning a required shared-document change to its Hub.
- [`product-truth.template.md`](product-truth.template.md) — product claims,
  capabilities, scope, and business language.
- [`product-tdd.template.md`](product-tdd.template.md) — a cross-unit
  technical contract when implementation surfaces cannot preserve it alone.

Do not copy the whole catalog. Replace admitted placeholders, remove optional
unadmitted sections, and keep durable claims in their canonical owner.
